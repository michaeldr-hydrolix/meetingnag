MeetingNag

A menu-bar app (⏰) that takes over your screen 1 minute before each meeting and keeps pinging until you click to confirm you've seen it.

  • Full-screen red alert on every display, with a live countdown and a Join button.
  • A sonar ping and “Meeting in less than a minute” spoken every 4 seconds, at full volume. Meeting titles are never spoken.
  • A log of every alert and how quickly you confirmed it.

Before you start: add your Google account in System Settings › Internet Accounts and turn on Calendars. MeetingNag reads meetings from there.

On the next screen you can also turn on Lid-closed mode, which is off by default.
